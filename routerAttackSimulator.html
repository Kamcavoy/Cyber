When off, the network name is hidden from casual discovery.
"Conceals the network name from casual discovery, reducing the casual attack surface and making your network less visible to opportunistic attackers."
Bottom line: "SSID hiding adds obscurity, not real security."
WEP is obsolete. WPA2-AES is the recommended baseline.
"WEP: Uses weak, easily crackable encryption keys. Can be bypassed by attackers in minutes."
"WPA2: Employs robust AES encryption for strong data protection. Highly secure when used with a strong, complex passphrase."
Best practice: "Always configure your network to use WPA2-Personal (or Enterprise) with AES encryption, or upgrade to WPA3 for enhanced security."
12+ characters, mixed case, numbers & symbols.
"Choose unique passphrases that are long (at least 12 characters), mix uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols."
Weak passphrases are matched quickly by offline dictionary attacks.
PIN-based pairing is brute-forceable. Best left disabled.
"PINs are vulnerable to brute-force attacks. Often enabled by default, creating a significant backdoor."
Best practice: "Disable WPS on routers to prevent attackers from gaining easy access through this weak point."
Allowlist of known devices. Easily spoofed, but adds friction.
"The router maintains a list of approved MAC addresses. Only devices on this list can connect."
"Easy to spoof (attackers can mimic MAC addresses). Not practical for large networks."
Limitation: "Both methods can be bypassed by skilled attackers using MAC spoofing or network scanning tools."
Isolates customers from internal café systems.
"Isolate guest networks from critical internal systems to limit potential breaches and contain threats."
Outdated firmware carries known exploits.
"Regularly check for and apply firmware updates from your router manufacturer to patch known vulnerabilities and improve performance."
Default credentials are the first thing attackers try.
"Default admin credentials allow unauthorised access to router settings for malicious changes."
Recommendation: "Change to a strong, unique administrator password."
